My Hair Care Routine

I honestly don't have a standard hair care routine, but here are a few tips I use:

  1. Wash hair every 3–4 weeks depending on if I have braids.
  2. Use a trusted shampoo brand.
  3. Try to use less heat on your hair.
  4. I use MPL Plus and Dark and Lovely products.
  5. Protect hair with braids, twists, or wigs.
  6. Use wide-tooth combs to reduce breakage.
  7. Be consistent with the hair products you use.
  8. Avoid relaxing or blow-drying your hair more than twice a year if you want a natural look.

Styling tips

  • Avoid elastic bands
  • Use wool
  • Scrunchies for cuteness
  • Use bows and pearly hair bands
  • Do edges with every hairstyle
  • Always make your hair stand-out
  • Match your hair ties with your outfits
  • Explore colors
