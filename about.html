About My Hair

I have natural hair and my goal is to grow it while keeping it healthy. I focus on moisture retention, protective styling and reducing breakage.

My hair journey has taught me patience, consistency and proper hair care. Taking care of 4 something hair is a lot of work but i have hard to mantain mine by doing all it takes trust me when i say i have tried it all, rice waters, hair mayonnaise, avocado, onion and all hair mosturises i could buy.

In 2017 December i had to do a big chop to my relaxed hair that was damaged and was forced to start over. Not having much options i did the big chop and started over with a mzansi known 'Chiskop'. The next year i was going to Grade 9 in a new school so i fe safer as no one in the new school knew how my hair was or how i looked, they know the 'me' they would see so i confidently rocked my short hair to a new school. I was so impatient with my hair and wanted it to grow so i can style it and braid it. When my patience ran out i started searching for products to make my hair curly from Clicks. I found Clicks branded products for curls and started using them on my short "not growing" hair not knowing how they would react to my hair.

My hair didnt become curly but instead it started breaking when i comb it. I had to stop using the products and look for better ones to avoid hair loss and breakage. I tried a bunch of hair foods from different brands until i got to Black chick hair food. It was good because my hair was not breaking anymore but i wasnt satisfied with its results as i wanted an afro with immediate effect.

I was still searching for the perfect hair products for my recovering afro and started searching on YouTube and bumped into home remedies that grow natural hair. Rice water and onion was the first 2 i tried. Drained rice water after cooking rice and washed my hair with it but saw no growth then proceeded to onion and garlic crushed and rubbed on scalp and oh my it made my hair smell bad for about 2 weeks!

Moved on to the next victim 'hair mayonnaise', couldnt buy one so made one at home and it was good because i wanted to make my afro be an afro after i had blown out my hair. So i countinued to buying one and also realised the MPL products that i bought to try. The two worked great for my hair but eventually i stopped using the mayonnaise and stuck to MPL oil and hair food. I have always believed braids also protect the hair so i taught myself how to braid my own hair and started doing my own hair and avoided being touched by many hands at salons. Grew my hair and faded my hairline, felt like its one after the other so I decided on installations so wig lines so i can install and grow my hairline back. Eventually fought the fear of hair dye and dyed parts of my hair caramel blonde and loved it down! and had it for about 3 months and decided why not dye the whole head? So me being "dora the explorer" I decided to dye my whole head caramel blonde and instatntly didnt know how i felt about it because im a shy face with a brave hair color but after taking it out for a walk, I loved it. I fell inlove with my hair colour and now stressed because my natural hair colour is growing and i do not want to dye my hair any further so i keep my natural hair in braids and wig lines.

My hair is my pride and i love it so much because it always boosts my confidence when my shyness creeps out!
