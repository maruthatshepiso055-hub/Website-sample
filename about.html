About My Hair

I have natural hair and my goal is to grow it while keeping it healthy. I focus on moisture retention, protective styling, and reducing breakage.

My hair journey has taught me patience, consistency, and proper hair care. Taking care of 4C hair is a lot of work, but I have worked hard to maintain mine. Trust me when I say I have tried it all: rice water, hair mayonnaise, avocado, onion treatments, and almost every hair moisturiser I could buy.

In December 2017, I had to do a big chop on my relaxed hair after it became damaged. Starting over was not easy, but it was necessary. In 2018, I started Grade 9 at a new school and felt more confident because nobody knew how I looked before the big chop. I embraced my short natural hair and began my journey from scratch.

I was impatient and wanted my hair to grow quickly so I could style and braid it. I started searching for products that would make my hair curly and tried several products from Clicks. Unfortunately, they did not work for my hair and instead caused breakage. I stopped using them and began searching for healthier alternatives.

After trying different hair foods, I discovered Black Chick Hair Food, which helped reduce breakage. However, I still wanted more growth and volume, so I started researching natural remedies on YouTube.

Rice water and onion treatments were among the first remedies I tried. While they did not produce the results I hoped for, they taught me that not every trend works for everyone. The onion treatment, however, left my hair smelling strong for weeks!

Later, I experimented with homemade hair mayonnaise and eventually switched to MPL Hair Oil and MPL Hair Food. These products worked well for me and became part of my routine. I also learned how to braid my own hair, which helped me avoid excessive manipulation and save money on salon visits.

Over time, I explored protective styles, wig installations, and eventually hair colour. I dyed sections of my hair caramel blonde and loved the result so much that I later dyed my entire head. Although I enjoyed the colour, I eventually decided not to continue dyeing my hair and now prefer protective styles that allow my natural colour and texture to thrive.

My hair is one of my greatest sources of confidence. It represents growth, patience, self-expression, and the lessons I have learned throughout my journey.
